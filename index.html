GOOD TO KNOW

Your search, your choice.

The extension redirects searches from supported sites to the engine you choose. Chrome's displayed search setting stays the same. Searches you make directly on a supported site are redirected too.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Good to know

Will my choice stay saved after I restart Chrome?

Yes. Your selected engine is stored locally in the browser and restored the next time you open the popup.

Does it work with address bar searches?

Yes, when Chrome's configured search engine is one of the supported options. The extension redirects its results page to the engine you selected.

Do I need an account?

No. You can choose an engine and search without signing up.
