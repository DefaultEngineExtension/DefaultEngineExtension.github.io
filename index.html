A QUICK NOTE

A switch for your searches.

The extension redirects search results from supported sites to your chosen engine. It also works when you search directly on those sites. The search engine shown in Chrome’s settings stays unchanged.

A FEW ANSWERS

Good to know.

Will my choice stay saved?

Yes. Your selection is stored locally in Chrome and restored after browser restarts.

Does it work from the address bar?

Yes, when Chrome’s current search provider is one of the nine supported engines. Other providers and unsupported search URLs are not redirected.

Do I need an account?

No. Install the extension, choose an engine, and start searching.

Does this demo change my search engine?

No. The preview only demonstrates the interface. Install the extension to redirect your searches.
